4. Strategic Differences & Egypt Operational Constraints

1. Why Serper.dev is 10x More Capital Efficient

• Unit cost: $0.0010 vs $0.0100 per query. A 50 EGP Basic plan user consumes only 0.75 EGP of search budget instead of 7.50 EGP.
• Prepaid flexibility: $50 gives 50,000 queries (~2,500 EGP) with a 6-month expiry. If app activity dips in month 1, your balance does not vanish (unlike SerpApi’s monthly recurring fee which expires unused searches).

2. SerpApi: Feature-Rich but High Monthly Floor

• Strengths: Highly polished UI, built-in search tester, and comprehensive documentation.
• Drawbacks: $50/mo minimum for only 5,000 queries. If you exceed 5,000 queries, overage is expensive unless upgrading to the $100/mo (15k) plan. For a startup in Egypt, paying $50/month continuously creates fixed FX burn.

3. Egypt CBE Regulations & Payment Practicalities

• Debit Cards Blocked: Egyptian Central Bank (CBE) prohibits foreign currency purchases on local debit cards.
• Required Setup: Use an Egyptian Credit Card (international limit: $50–$250/mo) or a virtual USD card (RedotPay / Elevate / Payoneer). With Serper, a single $50 charge lasts 3–6 months, avoiding monthly card limit rejections.
