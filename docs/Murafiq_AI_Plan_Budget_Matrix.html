1. Wardrobe Classification Invariant

Classifying uploaded garments runs asynchronously in BullMQ. Because it runs strictly once per garment and caches normalized attributes in MongoDB, classifying 50 items costs only 1.72 EGP, leaving chat reasoning free forever.

2. Safe Unit Economics on Basic

By capping the Basic tier (50 EGP) at 5 messages/day, 15 searches, and 4 try-ons, the plan achieves a guaranteed +31.5% profit margin (+15.73 EGP) even when paying full out-of-trial rates from query #1.

3. Free Trial Guard & Search Buffer

Capping Free tier at 10 lifetime messages per account cuts CAC to 1.22 EGP ($0.025 USD) and eliminates bot abuse. Additionally, Google AI Studio's 5,000 free monthly search queries further elevate Basic gross margins up to +45% to +55%.
