1. Wardrobe Classification

Garments are classified asynchronously via BullMQ. Because it runs strictly once per garment and caches normalized attributes in MongoDB, classifying 25 items costs only 0.88 EGP, leaving chat reasoning free forever.

2. Unit Economics on Basic

Capping the Basic tier (50 EGP) at 5 msgs/day, 15 searches, and 4 try-ons with Serper delivers a solid +50.3% profit margin (+25.15 EGP), protecting company cash flow.

3. Zero Try-On Leak on Free

Disabling Virtual Try-On on the Free tier sets customer acquisition cost (CAC) strictly to 1.25 EGP ($0.025 USD) for 10 lifetime chat messages and 7 wardrobe slots, eliminating trial GPU waste.

4. Serper 10x Advantage

Using Serper.dev ($0.001/query) instead of SerpApi ($0.010) or Google Grounding ($0.0153) keeps search costs under 2.25 EGP / month on Basic, saving thousands of pounds monthly at scale.
