4. Strategic Safeguards & Capital Protections

By removing Virtual Try-On from the Free tier (0 try-ons), free trial acquisition cost (CAC) dropped from 3.07 EGP to 1.25 EGP, completely blocking bot accounts and non-paying users from burning GPU compute.

2. Clean Currency Bounds

At 1 USD = 50.00 EGP, subscription prices align cleanly ($1.00 Basic, $3.00 Mid, $5.00 Pro, $10.00 Enterprise), making budgeting and margin calculations perfectly predictable.

3. Serper 10x Arbitrage

Using Serper.dev ($0.001/query) instead of SerpApi ($0.010) or Google Grounding ($0.0153) keeps external shopping search costs to under 2.25 EGP / month on Basic, yielding a +50.3% net profit margin.

Even at 4,500 monthly messages and 10 try-ons, the Enterprise plan remains positive (+17.20 EGP) in the absolute worst-case scenario.
