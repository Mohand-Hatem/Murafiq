1. The GPT-6 Luna Text Multiplier

At $0.10 in / $0.50 out per 1M tokens, GPT-6 Luna drops text reasoning costs by 63.5%. On Pro and Enterprise tiers where clients exchange 1,400 to 2,500 messages monthly, monthly text costs drop from 137–245 EGP to just 50–89 EGP.

2. Try-On Economics Divergence

While OpenAI dominates text pricing, GPT-Image-2 is 58% more expensive for virtual try-on (~2.60 EGP vs. ~1.65 EGP for Gemini Nano Banana 2 Lite). Using OpenAI for image generation diminishes its text savings on lower tiers.

3. Wardrobe 50% Storage Buffer

By enforcing active closet items at 50% of vision classification capacity (e.g. 25 items stored vs. 50 scans), Murafiq guarantees users a 2x re-upload/photo swap buffer while keeping BullMQ vision costs under 1.8 EGP / user permanently.

4. The Winning Hybrid Strategy

Routing Chat & Vision through GPT-6 Luna while routing Try-On through Gemini Flash Image Lite unlocks the ultimate business model: +71% to +76% gross profit margins across all paid tiers without any quality compromises.
